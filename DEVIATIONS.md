# Deviations from PREREGISTRATION.md

Every departure from the frozen design is recorded here with a timestamp and a
reason, per PREREGISTRATION.md section 9. Deviations are disclosed in the paper.

---

## D1 — Sample size below pre-registered target

**Logged:** 2026-09-15
**Status:** binding

**Pre-registered:** ~100 validated GSM8K sham-peer items.

**Actual:** 43 validated items (`data/stimuli/sham_gsm8k.jsonl`, status `ok`);
15 items failed automated validation and were discarded.

**Reason:** the session-level inference budget for the platform LLM interface was
exhausted during stimulus construction. The natural-language peer stage completed for
nearly all attempted items but the executable-peer stage could not run, and items
require both halves to pass validation. This is an infrastructure constraint, not a
data-dependent choice.

**Why this is not selection on outcomes:** stimuli were constructed and frozen
*before* any agent ran against them, so no outcome data existed at the time items
were retained or discarded. Discards were made solely by the pre-registered automated
gates (W != gold, both prose peers assert W, both peer programs execute and print
exactly W). No item was inspected for its effect on the result, and no item was
repaired by hand.

**Consequence:** reduced statistical power for the primary estimand. Disclosed in the
Limitations section, where a null result is explicitly framed as inconclusive rather
than as evidence of absence.

## D2 — Agent roster remains single-family

**Logged:** 2026-09-15
**Status:** binding

**Context:** additional provider credentials (OpenAI, Gemini, xAI, DeepSeek,
Moonshot) became available before the primary run, making a genuinely cross-family
roster technically possible.

**Decision:** keep the pre-registered single-family roster.

**Reason:** the change was considered and declined on deadline-risk grounds, not
scientific ones. Three additional provider integrations, answer-parsing surfaces and
rate-limit behaviours would have been introduced on the submission date. The primary
contrast is within-agent, so a single-family roster affects generality rather than
internal validity.

**Disclosure:** stated in Limitations as the study's most serious external-validity
threat and the most direct extension. Recorded here because the option existed and
was refused, so that the choice is visible rather than implicit.

## D3 — agent_B (claude-sonnet-4-5) removed from the roster
**When:** 2026-09-15, after the first execution pass, before any outcome quantity
was computed.

**What happened.** In the first pass over the 43 stimulus items, agent_B returned
`stop_reason="refusal"` with visible output truncated mid-sentence on 74 of 149
calls: 50/82 in the code medium (61%) and 24/67 in prose (36%). agent_A and
agent_C returned 0 refusals in 215 calls. Inspection of a refused record showed
the truncated text was an ordinary GSM8K arithmetic solution (gas mileage), cut
off after 98 output tokens. The pipeline's automatic retries did not clear it —
agent_B accumulated 82 records for 43 items and still ended with 50 refusals — so
the behaviour is persistent for this model/interface combination rather than
stochastic.

**Final log totals (added at write-up).** The counts above are those visible at the
decision point (first pass, 74/149). After the pipeline's automatic retries the
committed log `data/runs/conformity_v1.jsonl` contains 189 calls for this agent of
which 75 carry `stop_reason="refusal"`; the two retained agents contribute 415 calls
with 0 refusals. The paper quotes the final log totals, generated by
`src/make_tables.py` so they cannot drift from the log; this entry keeps the
first-pass counts because those, not the totals, were the basis for removal.

**Why this is a response-handling artifact, not a model refusal.** The content is
benign, the same model family answers the same items without incident at other
tiers, and the truncation is mid-generation rather than a declining message.

**Why removal rather than retry or imputation.** Retries were already exhausted.
Imputing or hand-repairing the truncated answers is forbidden by the
pre-registration. Keeping agent_B would be worse than dropping it: the refusal
rate is strongly DIFFERENTIAL across the two media (61% code vs 36% prose), and
the medium contrast is the primary estimand, so differential missingness by
medium would bias the very quantity the study reports.

**Basis for the decision.** `stop_reason` and truncation patterns only. No
harmful-conformity rate, no per-arm accuracy, and no value of the primary
estimand had been computed at the time of the decision. The roster change is
recorded in `src/run_conformity.py` with the counts above.

**Effect on the study.** The agent roster is two models (claude-haiku-4-5,
claude-opus-4-5) instead of three, reducing the number of item-agent pairs and
therefore statistical power. It also narrows an already-narrow model roster,
which is reported in Limitations as an external-validity threat. agent_B's
records remain in the run log; analysis is restricted to the final roster.

## D4 — chunk-major execution order
**When:** 2026-09-15, same pass, before any outcome quantity was computed.

The runner originally executed phase by phase across all items. Measured cost was
~4,200 tokens per call for the retained agents, and 1.12M of the session's ~2.0M
inference budget was already spent, so the remaining ~276 calls (~1.16M tokens)
could not all complete. Under phase-major order a budget cutoff would have left
every item with initial answers and missing revisions — i.e. zero fully-observed
item-agent pairs and no analysable dataset.

Execution was therefore restructured to complete all six phases for a chunk of
items before starting the next chunk (`src/drive_chunks.py`), with a hard token
stop below the ceiling. Items are processed in frozen-sample order, so truncation
is by budget position, not by outcome: no item's responses are inspected before
deciding whether to run it.

This changes only the ORDER of execution and the achieved sample size. It does not
change any prompt, arm, outcome definition, exclusion rule, or test. The realised
number of items is reported as-is, and the shortfall against the pre-registered
target is carried in Limitations together with D1.

## D5 — pre-registered secondary pools (H3) not run; difficulty screening reported instead
**When:** 2026-09-15, after the primary GSM8K run and after its initial accuracy
was known.

**Pre-registered:** H3 compared conformity across symbolically checkable (GSM8K)
and non-checkable (ARC-Challenge, MMLU-Pro) domains in the prose medium.

**Actual:** H3 was not tested. No pressure condition was run on any pool other
than GSM8K. What was run instead is an item-pool *difficulty* screening, reported
in Table~\ref{tab:pools}: initial (pre-pressure) accuracy on GSM8K, on GSM-Hard,
and on a quantitative MMLU-Pro subset.

**Why.** The primary run returned 100% initial accuracy on GSM8K in both media
for the retained roster (86/86 per medium). At ceiling the beneficial-revision arm
is empty by construction, so the design can observe harm but not benefit. Two
harder pools were screened to find one with headroom. GSM-Hard was also at or near
ceiling (100% prose, 90% code). A quantitative MMLU-Pro subset did show headroom
(2/4 per medium) but was not run under pressure: the validated pipeline emitted a
numeric-answer format rule and no option list, so multiple-choice prompting had to
be implemented (letter format rules, letter-aware program validation, sham
multiple-choice peers). That implementation is in the repository and validated on a
small run, but the sham-peer stage completed for only 11 of 16 attempted items
before the session inference ceiling made a six-phase run impossible within the
submission window.

**Selection discipline.** Pools were screened on *measured initial accuracy*, which
is observable before any pressure condition and therefore before any conformity
outcome exists. GSM-Hard items were additionally filtered on ground-truth sanity
(positive-integer targets only) — a property of the item label, not of model
behaviour. Every pool screened is reported here and in the paper, including the
one that was abandoned; none was dropped for its effect on the result.

**Consequence.** H3 is reported as untested, not as null. The saturation finding is
reported as a result in its own right and as the binding limitation on the primary
estimand's precision.

**Implementation note (not an outcome-dependent change).** The multiple-choice
prompting path, the letter-aware execution-consistency check, and the terminal-failure
guard in the stimulus generator were added after the primary run. They do not touch
the GSM8K numeric path: the numeric format rules and parser are unchanged, so the
reported run remains byte-reproducible.
